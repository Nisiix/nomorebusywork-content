Privacy Policy

No More Busywork (@nomorebusywork on Instagram) · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the No More Busywork Instagram account and its messaging tool ("NoMoreBusywork Publisher") handle, and why. The tool only works with our own Instagram account.

What we collect

  • When you comment on one of our posts or send us a direct message: your Instagram-scoped user ID, your username, the text of the comment or message, and its time.
  • When you ask for a free resource by keyword (for example PACK, PROMPTS or TOOLS): whether you follow @nomorebusywork, as reported by Instagram, so we can send follower-only resources.

We do not collect passwords, payment details, contacts, location or any information from outside Instagram.

How we use it

  • To reply to your comment or message and send you the resource you asked for.
  • To avoid sending you the same message twice.
  • To count, in aggregate, which resources people ask for, so we know what to make next.

We never sell or share your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and for how long

The records are stored in a private database hosted by Supabase (EU region) and are accessible only to the account owner. We keep them for up to 12 months, then delete them.

Deleting your data

Send a direct message to @nomorebusywork with the words DELETE MY DATA. We delete every record linked to your account within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @nomorebusywork.

No More Busywork is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.

# @nomorebusywork: collaboration target list

Researched 15 Oct 2026. The account is new (0 followers) and posts copy-paste AI prompts and workflows for small business owners and non-technical professionals in the US and UK.

**What we can offer partners**
- Co-authored carousels or Reels as Instagram Collab posts, so both accounts appear as authors and the post shows on both grids.
- Free prompt packs for their audience. The free 25-prompt pack (keyword PROMPTS) and the extended 21-prompt pack (keyword PACK) already exist in `offers/`, and a co-branded version can be made for any partner.
- Guest "template" posts: a ready-to-use prompt template written for their niche and credited to them.

**How the follower counts were checked**
- Instagram itself can't be fetched from this environment, so handles were verified through third-party sources.
- *creatordb* gives dated snapshots, and that date is shown in the table.
- *Social Blade* gives live counts with no timestamp; those figures were read on 15 Oct 2026.
- Other sources were creator websites, podcast show notes that link the Instagram profile, the Modash and creatorflow.so lists (Sep 2026), and a public media kit.
- "unverified" means the handle was confirmed but the follower count wasn't.
- No private contact details are included. The only email listed is a public partnership address from a public media kit.

**Priority:** A = start here (strong fit and likely to reply). B = good fit. C = stretch, meaning a larger account or a brand, so expect a lower reply rate.

## Caveats
- **Follower counts are approximate.** Social Blade figures were read on 15 Oct 2026 but have no timestamp on the page. creatordb snapshots are dated, and some of their "combined" totals mix Instagram and YouTube.
- **Some handles were confirmed only through a podcast or website link,** not by opening Instagram: #11, #19 and #29. Open each profile before sending to check it's active and still on topic.
- **Reply rates will be low at 0 followers.** Publish at least 6–9 strong posts and both prompt packs first, and lead with "I'll draft it, you approve it."
- **Collab posts need the partner to accept the invite** in the Instagram app. Bring finished drafts so all they have to do is accept.
- **Brands (#32–#39) often have partnership forms or agencies.** A DM may be routed elsewhere, so treat these as stretch attempts.
- **Finance and tax collabs (#9, #28, #29) should carry a "not financial/tax advice" note,** and the partner should approve the wording.
